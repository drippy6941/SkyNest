Frequently Asked Questions

How can we help you?

Find answers to common questions about accommodation, packages and facilities at SkyNest Co-Living Spaces.

What accommodation packages are available?

SkyNest offers Premium Stay, Standard Stay, Short-Term Stay and Custom Packages. Each option is designed for different accommodation needs and preferences.

What is included in Premium and Standard Stay?

Premium Stay includes a private studio, weekly cleaning, utilities, internet and access to all facilities.

Standard Stay offers a private bedroom in a shared apartment, fortnightly cleaning, utilities, co-working spaces and shared kitchen and lounge access.

Can I stay for a short period?

Yes, Short-Term Stay offers flexible accommodation for 1 to 4 weeks. It includes a furnished room, utilities and limited access to shared facilities.

What facilities does SkyNest provide?

SkyNest provides high-speed Wi-Fi, co-working spaces, communal kitchens, laundry rooms, a rooftop garden, gym and yoga studio, social lounge and study pods.

The building also provides 24/7 security and key-card access for residents.

How can I arrange a custom package?

Custom Packages are available for corporate bookings, long-term stays and residents with accessibility needs.

Contact SkyNest to discuss your requirements and available options.
